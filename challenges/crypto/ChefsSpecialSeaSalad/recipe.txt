How to make the Chef's Special Sea Salad.

Take your raw ingredients and put them in a bowl.

Then do these steps in order :
    - Make the stock base from the 8 octopus legs.
    - Take a big wooden spoon and make 13 rotations.
    - Use the Twofish in the drawer to get some key ingredients : cooked_shark_fin and grilled_fish_eye.
    - Do not touch anything else !
    - Then feed everybody from the 85 portions you have.
